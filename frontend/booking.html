What you can submit

Event basics, guest scale, location, budget range, requested services, and consultation preferences.

What happens next

Your booking starts in `REQUEST_SUBMITTED` and becomes the anchor for reviews, consultations, quotes, and agreements.

What gets preserved

Draft details stay in browser local storage until you submit or clear them by replacing the form.
